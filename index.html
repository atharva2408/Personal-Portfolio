A little about me

I’m Atharva Yanna, a software engineer who enjoys turning complex problems into practical, scalable software.

I’m an Associate Consultant at Emergys Solutions. I work across the full stack, building and improving production applications with React.js, Redux, Node.js, Express.js and SQL. That work has taught me to look past writing code: understand the problem first, design an efficient solution, then build something that makes a real difference.

Beyond full-stack development I’m deeply interested in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Generative AI. I’ve explored them through intelligent workflows, LLM-powered applications, computer vision and data-driven systems, and I like experimenting with emerging technology to see what real problems it can solve.

I believe good engineering is more than making things work. It’s making them reliable, efficient, maintainable and meaningful. Whether I’m building a backend service, tuning a database query or trying out an AI application, I enjoy the process of learning, building and improving.

Always curious. Always building. Always learning.
